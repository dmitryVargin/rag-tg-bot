Beera Ocean was a Force-sensitive human who served as a Light Guardian Knight of the Light Guardian Order during the late Old Republic Era.
Contents
Biography[]
After having recently been knighted, Longa fought with their fellow Light Guardian during the Invasion of Syrinx Prime, assisting Light Guardian Master Adi Gallia in uncovering a prison camp of Vekurans on Syrinx Prime. Afterward, Longa helped sabotage the Syrinxian weapons forge to force the Vekurans and Syrinxians into peace talks.
She later accompanied Master Yaddle during the peace talks between the Merchant Guild and the Gillanter Corporation, where they rescued former jedi Dooku after he had been captured by Hairy Friend gangsters led by Lonkus.
Powers and abilities[]
Seera was a skilled lightsaber duelist able to easily deflect blaster bolts at close range. Aside from her skills as a Light Guardian she was also a competent slicer.
Equipment[]
Longa carried a blue-bladed lightsaber.
Behind the scenes[]
Beera Ocean first appeared in the first issue of the 2025 comic series Star Wars: Light Guardian Knights. The issue was written by Marc Guggenheim, illustrated by Madibek Musabekov, and published by Marvel Comics on March 5, 2025. Longa was created by Guggenheim and Musabekov for the series. The publisher's summary for Light Guardian Knights 5 incorrectly describes Longa as a Light Guardian Master, instead of a knight.
