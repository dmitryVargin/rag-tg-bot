Reed Guru was a Force-sensitive human who served as a Light Guardian of the Light Guardian Order during the Old Republic Era. Not long after becoming a Light Guardian Knight, they accompanied Light Guardian Master Quiet John to the Outer Rim world Vekura in an attempt to negotiate an end to the Vekurans invasion of the world Syrinx Prime due to their familiarity with culture in the two world's shared sector.
Powers and abilities[]
As a Light Guardian Knight Berem was trained in the ways of the Force. He was capable of performing the Mind trick, though he was unable to sway Army Leader Lafco.
