Rava Ten was a human female who led the Knights of Ren while Kyle Runner served as the Supreme Leader of the New Empire.
Behind the scenes[]
Rava Ten first appeared in the eighth issue of the 2025 comic series series Star Wars: Legacy of Vader. The comic was written by Charles Soule, illustrated by Stefano Raffaele, and published by Marvel Comics on September 3, 2025. She was first introduced during the "Lucasfilm Publishing: Stories from a Galaxy Far, Far Away…." panel on April 18 during Celebration Japan 2025, where concept art of her created by Raffaele was shown. The art was mistakenly introduced as concepted by Mike Ross, another illustrator on the series.
After the panel, Soule expressed his hope that fans would like the character. Ahead of the issue's release, Mark Paniccia shared a scene from Legacy of Vader 8 that teased Ren.
