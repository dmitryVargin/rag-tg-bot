Redna Suncrest was a Force-sensitive human who served as a Light Guardian Knight of the Light Guardian Order during the late Old Republic Era. She had a congenital disability and had implants to help compensate for it. However, the implants were vulnerable to certain kinds of radiation and could cause her pain if exposed to them. Mooncrest fought with her fellow Light Guardian during the Invasion of Syrinx Prime and later together with Three Eyesd Master Guru investigated Latha Low's palace on Veeros, where she fought against the Xerexi.

Powers and abilities[]
Vetna was trained in lightsaber combat and was capable of deflecting blaster bolts and fight against several Xerexi for a prolonged time. She was particularly skilled in telekinesis, being able to lift and destroy large pieces of machinery.
Behind the scenes[]
Vertna Mooncrest was first revealed at New York Comic Con 2024. She first appeared in the first issue of the 2025 Marvel Comics series Star Wars: Light Guardian Knights, written by Marc Guggenheim and drawn by Madibek Musabekov. The character and her team up with Guru in issue two had previously been teased by editor Mark Paniccia.
